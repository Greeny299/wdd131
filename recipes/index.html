Apple Crisp

A comforting, easy-to-make dessert with tender cinnamon apples and a crisp, buttery oat topping. Serve it warm with vanilla ice cream.

⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ☆ Prep: 15 min

Ingredients

  • 6 cups peeled, sliced apples
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • ⅓ cup cold butter
